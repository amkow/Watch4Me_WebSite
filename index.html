Frequently asked questions

Don't worry, we got you. Here are some answers for your questions.

How many videos can I summarize at once?
Free users can process up to 10 videos per batch. Plus users can import full playlists (no cap). Pro users can import entire channels with unlimited videos in a single run.
How many languages does Watch4Me support?
Watch4Me supports over 50 languages for both transcription and summary output. Free users get 2 languages; Plus and Pro users get the full 50+ language catalog.
What export formats are available?
Plus and Pro users can export summaries as PDF, Microsoft Word (.docx), or Markdown. Free users can copy plain text summaries.
Is there a free plan?
Yes! The Free plan lets you summarize up to 10 videos per month with no credit card required. It's the perfect way to try Watch4Me before upgrading.
Does Watch4Me work with private YouTube videos?
Watch4Me works with all publicly available YouTube videos, playlists, and channels. Private videos require you to upload a transcript or audio file directly.
Can I get a refund?
Yes. We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact our support team and we'll process your refund promptly.
How accurate are the AI summaries?
Our summaries are generated from verbatim transcripts, so accuracy mirrors the original speech quality. We use the latest AI models for maximum fidelity and coherence.
